Phone pairing — standalone Radicale

Fill in what you just set up, get a QR code for DAVx5 (Android). This page runs entirely in your browser — nothing typed here is sent anywhere, it only builds a link locally.

QR appears here once host, username and password are filled in.
—
⚠️ The link and QR code both carry the password in plain text — this isn't a reference to the password, it is the password, readable by anyone who sees the image or the text. Scan it straight into DAVx5 and then close this page; don't screenshot the QR, and don't paste the copied link into chat, email, or a notes app (those commonly sync to the cloud or get logged, which can carry the password well outside your LAN without anyone specifically trying to leak it). If you're ever unsure who's seen one, change that user's password (server/standalone/README.md has the steps) — old links stop working once the password changes.
No network calls happen on this page — everything (including the QR image) is generated locally by the bundled vendor/qrcode.min.js. Safe to open with no internet connection.
